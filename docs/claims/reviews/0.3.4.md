## Findings

### 1. docs/API-STABILITY.md:169 — spoken and dotted addresses without a named ending list

- Claim: "spoken `et` / `at` and `nokta` addresses and four or more dotted
  labels whose first two are letters only"
- Mechanism: `findSpokenEmails` and `findDottedEmails` in `src/mask/speech.ts`
  keep a run only when the last label is in `DOMAIN_ENDINGS`
- Why it falls short: `ayse.yilmaz.example.xyzzy` is four dotted labels
  whose first two are letters only and is not masked — `xyzzy` is not in
  the list
- Disposition: reworded in `bf49021` to name `DOMAIN_ENDINGS` in
  `src/mask/speech.ts` and that an address ending in anything else is not
  masked by these rules

### 2. CHANGELOG.md (0.3.4, previous wording) — "an amount written with dots or commas"

- Claim: "It leaves an amount written with dots or commas"
- Mechanism: `findDigitRuns` masks a run of 7 or more digits; thousands
  separators (`1.250.000`, `1.250,00`) break the run, and fewer than 7
  digits before a decimal comma stay
- Why it falls short: `1234567,89` is written with a comma and is a run
  of 7 digits, so it is masked
- Disposition: reworded in `bf49021`

### 3. docs/API-STABILITY.md:169 — "an address ending in anything else is not masked"

- Claim: "ending in the fixed list `DOMAIN_ENDINGS` … (an address ending in
  anything else is not masked by these rules)"
- Mechanism: `findSpokenEmails` in `src/mask/speech.ts` takes the rightmost
  word in `DOMAIN_ENDINGS` on the nokta chain after `et` / `at`, not the
  chain's last word
- Why it falls short: `ayse et example nokta dev nokta jp` ends in `jp`,
  which is not in the list, and becomes `[MASKED_PII] nokta jp`
- Disposition: reworded in `026a6f4`: a dotted run's last label, and a spoken
  address read up to its last listed word

### 4. docs/API-STABILITY.md:169 — "under 7 digits before its decimal comma stays"

- Claim: "An amount … under 7 digits before its decimal comma stays"
- Mechanism: `findDigitRuns` reads each side of the comma as its own run
- Why it falls short: `0,1234567 BTC` has one digit before the comma and
  becomes `0,[MASKED_PII] BTC`
- Disposition: reworded in `026a6f4`: a decimal part of 7 or more digits is
  masked too

### 5. docs/API-STABILITY.md:169 — Turkish-specific letters on an English surface

- Claim: the first wording of this round wrote the mixed-form examples and
  the dotted capital I with Turkish-specific letters (U+015F, U+0130, U+0131)
- Mechanism: `test/locale_letters.mjs` and `test/claim_discipline.mjs` reject
  those letters on English claim surfaces; `test/pack_locale.mjs` rejects
  them in `dist/`
- Why it falls short: that wording failed `locale_letters` on this row, and
  `endingKey` with a literal U+0131 failed `pack_locale` on
  `dist/mask/speech.js`
- Disposition: reworded in `8336ca7` to ASCII local parts and to U+0130 /
  U+0131 by code point
