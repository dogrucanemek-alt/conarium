## Findings

### 1. LIMITATIONS.md:63 — an eleven-digit number stays readable

- Claim: "A joined landline (`siparis 2024 02321234567`), an eleven-digit number that fails the ID checksum, and a number with no separator before it (`siparis 202410000000146`) stay readable."
- Mechanism: the joined-number windows accept only parts that pass a checksum or are a Turkish mobile number.
- Why it falls short: run at the head, `siparis 2024 12345678901` comes back as `siparis [MASKED_PII]` in both paths: the frozen 0.2.54 steps mask it, so the sentence said less was masked than is.
- Disposition: reworded in `c529a1d` (the eleven-digit clause removed, in both languages).

### 2. LIMITATIONS.md:89 — custom patterns that can backtrack are refused

- Claim: "A custom pattern is refused when the policy loads if it can backtrack without bound: a quantified group that holds a quantifier or an alternation …, or two adjacent variable-width quantifiers over overlapping characters …"
- Mechanism: shape rules in `looksUnsafe`.
- Why it falls short: a third reading built `[0-9]{0,64}a?[0-9]{0,64}a?[0-9]{0,64}X`, which passed the rules and took 27 s on 16 000 digits; the same rules refused `(?:https?)?`, which 0.2.54 accepted. No list of shapes established "can backtrack without bound".
- Disposition: the mechanism changed (custom patterns run on a linear-time engine; the shape rules added in this release are gone, the 0.2.54 rules stay) and the section was rewritten in `b13a3e2`, in both languages.

### 3. LIMITATIONS.md:85 — what the 0.2.54 copy can hide that is not personal

- Claim: "Some text next to a masked value can then be hidden although it is not personal: a title without a dot (`Bay`, `Bayan`) next to a masked number, or a custom rule's label written literally in the source text."
- Mechanism: the belt masks every span the frozen 0.2.54 output implies; where that output fits the text in more than one way, the implied spans are the widest ones.
- Why it falls short: a pre-merge read found that the same title without a dot starting line after line makes the widest spans overlap across the whole run. Run at the head on the model path, `Sayin Ahmet`, `Sayin Mehmet`, `Sayin Ali` on three lines comes back as one mask (`Sayin [MASKED_PII]` and a line break); v0.2.54 gives two. The list named only the adjacent title and the literal label.
- Disposition: a sentence added in `ed149ef` (the English title spelled `Sayin` in `d2c7bf3`), in both languages, and narrowed in `fd560e2` and `baf3a9f`: the copy's output can fit those lines in more than one way, and the masks over them can then merge into one. The first wording said the region runs from the first name to the last; a second read showed that does not hold for every name shape, so the sentence no longer says where the region ends. Run at the head: `Bay Ab` on 2 340 lines gives one mask in 16 ms on the model path; the audit path keeps one mask for every one or two of those lines; a title followed by other lines (`Sayin Ahmet Bey,` then a sentence) gives the same output as v0.2.54 on the model path. The quadratic time and memory the read also found were fixed in `45caed5` (overlapping spans merge); the "up to about four times" sentence still holds on the inputs of that read (one megabyte of `Bay Ab` lines: 337 ms against 78 ms; 40 custom rules over dense matches: about three times).
