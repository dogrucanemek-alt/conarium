The release changes `boundAssignment` in `bin/conarium-reconcile.mjs`: the
patterns that draw and share an in-window Receipt with a pattern that draws,
over several objects, that a Receipt names in part are decided together. It
adds one conformance case, two tests in `test/reconcile_cli.test.mjs` and
`test/bound_assignment_brute.test.mjs`, which enumerates every assignment of
400 random inputs (up to three patterns of one or two objects each, over three
objects, up to three Receipt groups, a bound of 1 or 2) and compares
`boundAssignment` with the rule.

Each new sentence was set against what establishes it. "Seen failing" means
the test went red when the code it names was changed, and green again when it
was restored.

- "A statement is covered by one in-window Receipt that names all of its
  pattern's objects or by an in-window Receipt for each object": the rule the
  brute-force test enumerates, and `one receipt that names both objects of a
  join covers one statement of it`, `a join is covered by one receipt for each
  of its objects`.
- "The assignment is exact except for a pattern that draws, over several
  objects, where an in-window Receipt names one of its objects but not all of
  them": the
  brute-force test requires the same answer as the rule, both ways, on every
  input with no such pattern (more than 50 of the 400). Seen failing with
  every pattern treated as one a Receipt names in part.
- "Such a pattern draws only on the Receipts that name it whole, where there
  are any. Where there are none, it is drawn against each object's Receipts":
  `a join that some receipt names whole draws only on receipts that name it
  whole (LIMITATIONS.md)` and `a join no receipt names whole, named by
  receipts that overlap, counts a shared receipt once per object
  (LIMITATIONS.md)`.
- "Either way the assignment can show a pattern beside it covered when another
  maximum assignment would not": the brute-force test fails on 0.3.7's
  `boundAssignment`, and so do `a pattern beside a join that some receipt
  names whole is not matched when the join could take its receipt` and
  `conformance/cases/reconciliation/v2-multiplicity-join-neighbour.json`.
- "So the patterns that draw and share an in-window Receipt with such a
  pattern, directly or through other patterns that draw, are taken together":
  `a pattern on a receipt that a join could take for one of its objects is not
  matched beside the join` (the Receipt names one object of the join). Seen

## Findings

### 1. LIMITATIONS.md — which way 0.3.7's comparison errs

- Claim: "The comparison errs toward `indeterminate` in two cases. A pattern
  over several objects that some receipt in the window names whole draws only
  on the receipts that name it whole, even when the others would cover the
  rest." (0.3.7, also in `LIMITATIONS.tr.md` and `docs/RECEIPT-SPEC.md`).
- Mechanism: in 0.3.7 such a pattern's node drew only on the groups that name
  it whole.
- Why it falls short: that errs toward `indeterminate` for the pattern itself,
  not for the patterns beside it. With a bound of 1, Receipts naming customers
  and orders, customers, and orders, two statements of the join and one on
  orders, 0.3.7 reported the orders pattern `matched`; one maximum assignment
  covers both join statements and leaves the orders statement uncovered. An
  independent read of draft-08 found it; it was reproduced on the published
  0.3.7 package.
- Disposition: fixed in `db4941b`; the sentence is replaced in all three
  files.

### 2. docs/RECEIPT-SPEC.md — how far "errs only toward" is established

- Claim: "The comparison therefore errs only toward `indeterminate`."
- Mechanism: the argument in the comment on `boundAssignment` (a flow that
  covers every statement of a component is an assignment the rule accepts;
  otherwise every pattern in a component with a rough pattern is shared), and
  the brute-force test.
- Why it falls short: the test enumerates inputs of up to three patterns of
  one or two objects each, three objects and three Receipt groups. Patterns
  over three objects, and anything larger, rest on the argument.
- Disposition: accepted, with the test's inputs stated here.

### 3. docs/RECEIPT-SPEC.md — which patterns are taken together

- Claim: "So the patterns that share an in-window Receipt with such a pattern,
  directly or through other patterns, are taken together" (a draft of this
  release, also in `LIMITATIONS.md` and `LIMITATIONS.tr.md`).
- Mechanism: the components in `boundAssignment` are built over
  `result.reconciled` only.
- Why it falls short: a pattern with an object no in-window Receipt names
  shares a Receipt with its neighbours but draws on none, and is not taken
  together with them. With one Receipt naming customers, two statements of a
  customers-orders join and one on customers, the join is
  `observed-without-receipt` and the customers pattern stays `matched`. An
  independent read of the pull request text found it.
- Disposition: reworded in `438c920` to name the patterns that draw, in all
  three files.
